ORB5: first 5-min candle high/low → close beyond it, then a retest that closes beyond again → buy ATM CE (up) / PE (down). Stop = middle of that candle. At +30 pts book, stop to break-even, then trail 20 pts per 20 pts.
EMA9_21: the 9/21 cross only arms the setup (never an entry). Entry = a later candle whose wick retests the 9 EMA and whose body closes on the trend side (above for CE, below for PE); a wrong-side close is skipped. Stop = last 3 candles' low/high. At +30 pts book and stop to break-even; the rest exits only when a candle body closes across the 9 EMA (wicks don't count).
Each strategy on its own: trade 1 = 5 lots (book 3); win → done for the day; loss → trade 2 = 7 lots (book 5); 2 losses → done. No entries after 14:30, square-off 15:15.
