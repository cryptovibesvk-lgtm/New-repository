Day routing: NIFTY trades Mon, Tue, Fri · SENSEX trades Wed, Thu. Only that day's index takes trades.
ORB1 (1-min): first 1-minute candle (09:15) high/low → a 1-min close above the high buys ATM CE, below the low buys ATM PE. NIFTY: SL 20 index pts (~10 premium pts); +20 → SL −10, +30 → break-even, +40 → SL +10, then every +10. Qty 195, then 325 after a loss. SENSEX: SL 30 pts; +30 → break-even, +40 → SL +10, then every +10. Qty 200, then 300 after a loss.
EMA9_21 (5-min): cross arms the setup; entry on a later candle that wicks to the 9 EMA and closes on the trend side. Stop = last 3 candles' low/high. Book at +30 and stop to break-even; rest exits on a candle body close across the 9 EMA. 5 lots, then 7 after a loss.
Each strategy on its own: a win ends it for the day, two losses end it. No entries after 14:30, square-off 15:15. Option book ₹5L. Loss stops: ₹10,000 day, ₹15,000 week, ₹30,000 month. Premiums are Black-Scholes estimates from India VIX.
